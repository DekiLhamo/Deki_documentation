# What is Vinyl Cutting?
Vinyl cutting is a way to make custom stickers, signs, and graphics. 

A machine called a vinyl cutter uses a tiny blade to cut lines into a sticky sheet of vinyl without cutting through the paper on the back.  

<img class="profile-photo" src="https://drive.google.com/thumbnail?id=1l38BdCAa7nsZsp7uYX0Ub_N-nfQFhQ5n&sz=w400" alt="Profile Photo">

After the machine cuts your design, you peel away the extra vinyl. This step is called weeding. You are left with your sticker, which you can stick onto laptops, glass, or shirts. 

# Machine Parts
Before you start, learn the main parts of the vinyl cutter:

<img class="profile-photo" src="https://drive.google.com/thumbnail?id=1l38BdCAa7nsZsp7uYX0Ub_N-nfQFhQ5n&sz=w400" alt="Profile Photo">

1.Loading Lever: Unlocks and locks the vinyl in place. 

2.Pinch Rollers: Small wheels that push down on the vinyl to roll it straight.  

3.Guide Lines: Line marks on the machine to help you load your vinyl straight.  

4.Cutting Carriage: The moving block that holds the blade holder.  

5.Operation Panel: The buttons and screen used to set up the cutter.  

# Step-by-Step Instructions
## Step 1: Set Up the Blade 
Use a 45° blade for thin, standard vinyl.   

Use a 60° blade for thick vinyl or small, detailed cuts.   

Put the blade into the blade holder.   

Turn the top cap on the blade holder to set the depth. Only the tiny point of the blade tip should show.

 This tells the software how wide your vinyl is.   

## Step 4: Load the Vinyl

<img class="profile-photo" src="https://drive.google.com/thumbnail?id=1l38BdCAa7nsZsp7uYX0Ub_N-nfQFhQ5n&sz=w400" alt="Profile Photo">

Push the Loading Lever back to lift the rollers.   

Slide your vinyl sheet or roll into the machine from back to front, aligning it with the Guide Lines.   

Move the pinch rollers so they line up directly over the gritty metal rollers underneath the edges of your vinyl sheet.

Pull the Loading Lever forward to lock the vinyl sheet or roll in place.   

## Step 5: Start Cutting

To print the stciker/image, I then pressed ctrl + p.

<img class="profile-photo" src="https://drive.google.com/thumbnail?id=1b35-gSzAKTwklMLGHqggRCUbC6DPZvVv&sz=w400" alt="Profile Photo">


The machine will start cutting out your design.   

## Step 6: Weed Your Sticker
Unlock the lever and pull your vinyl sheet out.   

Use tweezers or a weeding tool to lift and peel away the extra vinyl you do not need.   

Carefully pull out small inside pieces (like the middle of letters).   

Put transfer tape over your clean design to move your new sticker to its surface.
