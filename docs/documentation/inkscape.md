## Draw the Shape: 
Select the Rectangle tool from the left toolbar, then click and drag on the canvas.

## Set the Outline: 
Open the Fill and Stroke panel. 

Turn Fill OFF and turn Stroke ON. 

Set the stroke width to Hairline (ideal for laser cutting).

## Adjust Dimensions & Corners: 
Double-click the rectangle's boundary line to open its settings. 

Adjust the W (Width) and H (Height). 

Increase Rx or Ry to round the corners.

## Add a Keyhole
Select the Circle tool and draw a small circle near one edge of your rectangle.

Set its Rx and Ry (radii) to make it a perfect, evenly sized circle.

## Add Text
Click the Text tool (the 'A' icon on the left), then click on the canvas and type your text.

Switch to the Select tool (arrow icon) to resize and position the text inside the keychain.

In the Fill and Stroke panel, keep Fill turned ON and turn Stroke OFF so the laser engraves the text instead of cutting through it.

## Trace and Add an Image.
1.Download your image and save it.

Import your image into Inkscape 

Right-click the image and select Trace Bitmap.

In the panel that opens on the right, select Brightness Cutoff and click Apply.

Drag the newly created vector trace off the original image, place it onto your keychain design, and resize as needed.
